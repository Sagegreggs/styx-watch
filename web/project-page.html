One scan, three answers
What you see

      

        
The river
Big transfers are main streams, smaller ones are streams and rivulets. When a payment splits across several wallets, each part becomes a tributary.
━━━━━━━ MAIN STREAM ⇢ out 155.51 BTC
    ├── tributary 80 BTC
    └── tributary 45.5 BTC

        
The lake
What the wallet holds becomes a body of water. A dry bed holds nothing. The Abyss of Tartarus holds more than ten times a main stream.
Reservoir: Lake Avernus · 18,420 SOL

        
The judgement
Every wallet it dealt with is checked against OFAC sanctions lists, the ScamSniffer blacklist and GoPlus risk flags.
Clean · Tainted · Damned

      

    

  


  

    

      
How strong is the current?
The depth gauge
A wallet's activity is graded by how many transactions it has made. Under 100 is weak. Over 1,000 earns its own name.

Getting it
Use it in PayBox
Styx Watch is a PayBox plugin entered in the MoonPay hackathon. Anyone can install it once PayBox approves it. Until then, the test console and river map show what it does.

      

        
Install
Find Styx Watch in PayBox's plugin list and turn it on.

        
Ask
Ask your agent to scan a wallet, or to show the great currents.

        
Decide
Read the judgement before you send anything. It never asks for your keys.

      

    

  






  

    
A Clean judgement means none of the listed sources flag the wallet. It is not a guarantee that a wallet is safe. Exchange labels come from public explorer name tags and do not prove who owns a wallet.
